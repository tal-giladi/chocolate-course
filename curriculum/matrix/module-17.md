## Statements without a direct source

- In-force status of the 2021 reform and the תשפ"ו amendment: Wikisource shows the amended text; IDI (2024) says most of the reform was not in force. Lessons say "not verified which parts apply today; ask the religious council".
- Kashrut fees for 2026 and mashgiach rates for a small chocolate kitchen: not found. 49 ₪/hour is labelled a 2019 average, not a price. The 2026 fee notice (~45 ₪/h via bad OCR, sources.md gaps) was not used.
- How the Gini ruling and the 2018 AG opinion (both about restaurants) apply to a manufacturer's packaged-product label: not verified; lessons send learners to a lawyer. Whether the private body's name may be stated: disputed (sources.md gap).
- Whether "פרווה"/"חלבי" alone on a label count as a kashrut representation: not ruled; course rule (same as 13.5) is to write them only per certificate.
- Badatz/private hechsher process and cost for small producers: no source; replaced with a question list.
- Whether a rented kitchen's certificate covers a tenant business: no source; lessons say ask the council in writing.
