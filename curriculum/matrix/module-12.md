## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Home vegan "milk" recipe (160 g 70% dark, 40 g cocoa butter, 50 g plant-drink powder, 50 g icing sugar) and its ~51% cocoa calculation: our formulation, derived from Chocolate Alchemy's fat guidance and Amatika's composition; not tested in a lab.
- Seeding the home vegan "milk" with 1% Mycryo at 33-34 °C: transferred from 03.4 (Cacao Barry milk values); allowing up to 8 min for the knife test with coconut powder: practice.
- Rice and oat drink powders are "usually much lower in fat" than coconut milk powder: tell learners to read the nutrition table; no single source opened.
- "A home blender is not built to grind to 20-30 µm": judgement.
- Blender ≤50 °C for the vegan "milk" base: from 03.2 milk melting range, not a vegan-specific source.
- Oat drink "mostly water, like milk (88%)": Callebaut gives milk; oat drink water computed from the label method in the lesson.
- Plant ganache storage (fridge, coconut ganache no longer than 06.4's cream ganache; oat ganache 2-3 days): no aw measurement found; conservative judgement.
- Tahini:dark 1:1 filling ratio, fills at 26-28 °C, keeps "weeks" at 14-18 °C like praline: our starting point by analogy with 07.2 / 05.2.
- Medjool dates "up to about two weeks" in a closed box: judgement (only Deglet Noor composition found; KSU dried-fruit aw band 0.60-0.65 via 06.4).
- Coconut caramel quantities (60 g sugar, 15 g glucose, 15 g water, 60 g coconut cream, 8 g coconut oil): scaled from 07.1; separation fixed with a stick blender is practice.
- Aquafaba nougat: no professional recipe with numbers found; lesson frames it as an experiment.
- Beetroot / freeze-dried berry powders as red colour without E120, carnauba as a plant wax: general knowledge, not sourced (carnauba mentioned only inside a quoted label).
- "Some plant spreads contain milk ingredients": tell learners to read labels; no specific product cited.
