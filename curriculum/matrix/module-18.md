## Statements without a primary source (flagged in the lessons)

- Contents of ת"י 1145, ת"י 650 part 4 and ת"י 1118 part 1: not read (standards not freely available). Lessons say so and send the question to the district health office.
- Whether the 2028 draft was adopted, and whether it changes the nutrition-labelling regulations: not verified.
- Whether the EU Annex V item 19 handcrafted exemption will apply in Israel: unknown; lessons tell learners to assume the table applies.
- "Chocolate gets two red symbols": our inference from thresholds + the three Valrhona sheets.
- How to measure x-height in Hebrew: no rule found; lesson gives a conservative house rule (נ/ס ≥1.2 mm in print; ~8 pt is "usually", not sourced).
- Exact wording of the official red symbols: not verified; SVG shows generic "סמל סוכר / סמל שומן רווי".
- "When a lab is needed" decision flow, two-condition trial design, five checkpoints, 1-5 score sheet: our method, built on Callebaut's principles (Callebaut gives no sampling intervals).
- Trademark check at רשות הפטנטים: general pointer, not opened.
- Israeli food-contact marking rule: not checked (EU 1935/2004 used as reference).
- Sugar-teaspoon rounding rules (reg. 4(a)(7)): not read in detail; worked example says "כ־10, לפי כללי העיגול".
- Sodium = salt ÷ 2.5: standard conversion, not from an opened source.
