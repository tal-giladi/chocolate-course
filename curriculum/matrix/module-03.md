## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Home seed quantity 100 g of 300 g (one third), ~70 g first: derived from King Arthur (25%) plus margin; not a manufacturer number.
- Seeding time 10-20 min and Mycryo cooling time 10-20 min for 300 g at 20-22 °C: estimates.
- Israeli summer kitchen 27-30 °C, countertop ~27 °C: general observation, not measured.
- Fat bloom "linked in research to the slow V to VI transition": supported by review snippets (EJLST) and Wikipedia's note that VI forms in storage; the review itself was not opened.
- Freezer or long fridge cooling causes cracks: CakeFlix says too cold causes cracking; "freezer" specifically is general practice.
- Plant-based "milk" chocolate (Callebaut NXT etc.): no manufacturer tempering curve found; lessons say "follow the pack".
- Mycryo kashrut and availability in Israel: not checked; lessons say read the package and fall back to seeding.
- Mycryo left in a hot car stops working: reasoning from the crystal science, not a manufacturer statement.
- A Callebaut product page (Greek) reportedly says 2% Mycryo; not opened, not used.

## Format decisions for later modules (pilot)

- `מה צריך` is a table `פריט | כמות לתרגול | כמות מלאה | הערה`, then bold-lead paragraphs (**אלרגנים:**, and a "why this quantity" note where relevant).
- `למה זה עובד` uses `###` sub-headings and names the source inline ("לפי Callebaut…") for every number; manufacturer ranges go in a table with the brand beside each range, plus "the curve on the pack wins".
- One `> [!WARNING]` at the top of `שלב אחר שלב`; maltitol always as `> [!CAUTION]` (or the full warning sentence) inside `### בריא יותר`.
- `### כשרות`: what to look for on the pack, the kashrut-guide link, "אם יש ספק, שואלים את הרב". No halachic conclusions (say "you can no longer know what's in the bag", not "it becomes dairy").
- First lesson of a practical module: `### לפני שמתחילים את המודול` inside `מה צריך` with "watching is not doing / no photo grading / keep a log".
- Practice reuses the same chocolate across lessons and the project (re-melt), so the whole module costs ~300-400 g.
- Project `בדיקה עצמית` table ends with a "בלי בזבוז" row.
