## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- All "when to start" lead times (8 weeks mould from abroad comes from 08.2 / where-to-buy; the rest — coins 2-3 weeks, hanukkiah 1-2 weeks, bark ≤2 weeks, stuffed dates 1-4 days, Purim 7-10 days, Pesach raw materials 4-6 weeks, bonbons 4-7 days, event 3-4 weeks tasting) are my planning judgement from shelf-life logic in 05.2/06.4.
- Hanukkiah design (base 16×4 cm, 5-6 mm; candles 3.5 cm, shamash 5 cm; weights 50-55 g) — my design; weights computed from volume × ~1.3 g/cm³.
- "Tempered chocolate softens around 28-30 °C" (10.3) and "loses shine a few degrees before 34 °C" (10.6) — general practice, not sourced.
- Stuffed-date shelf life "days to about a week" — inferred from KSU aw table + Callebaut nut-filling bloom; lesson says test it.
- Toffee for matzah to 145-150 °C, 60 g sugar / 50 g butter / ¼ tsp lemon — my recipe from standard toffee practice; 07.x sugar-stage chart supports the range.
- Icing sugar "sometimes contains starch", vanilla extract on grain-alcohol base needing KFP — general label-reading advice, not a ruling.
- "No KFP no-kitniyot couverture found" — based on references/kashrut-guide.md (2026-11-10, out of season).
- Honey 5-8% of ganache as a working range — derived from Callebaut's ~5.6%.
- Honey not for infants under 1 year — general food-safety guidance, not sourced here.
- Quantities per guest (2-3 / 4-5 units, favours 20-40 g, +10% reserve, +20% raw material) — practical judgement, stated as such in 10.6.
- Kashrut: tithes on Israeli fruit, kashering year-round utensils for Pesach, whether two chocolate items count as two kinds for mishloach manot, venues with kashrut certificates and outside food — all phrased as "ask the rabbi / ask the venue", no rulings.
- Burn first aid "cool running water 20 min" (10.4) — from NHS as cited in 08.3, not re-cited here.
