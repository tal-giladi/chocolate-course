## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- "Add 2-3% cocoa butter (6-10 g per 300 g) to lecithin-free chocolate": our calculation from Blommer's 0.3-0.4% ≈ >10x ratio, not a manufacturer instruction (said so in 14.1).
- Pumpkin/sunflower seeds as nut-free crunch; "some people are allergic to them": general knowledge.
- Wheat flour ≈ 8-10 g gluten per 100 g, so 1 g flour per kg ≈ 80-100 ppm: rough estimate, flagged in 14.2 as such.
- "Kosher for Passover ≠ gluten-free (matzah meal is wheat)": general knowledge.
- Which tools go in the "guest box" (silicone, wood, cloth, sponge, brushes, reusable piping bags, silicone moulds; never the praline food processor): judgement from material properties, said so in 14.3. Silicone moulds in the guest box is conservative.
- Cocoa-powder "visible trace" cleaning exercise: teaching proxy, explicitly not an allergen test.
- Emergency step (call MDA 101, guest uses own epinephrine as prescribed): general first-aid practice; FDA symptoms/anaphylaxis from 01.4.
- "Today there is no uniform allergen labelling obligation in Israel": from walla/maariv quoting MoH, not from the regulation itself (sources.md lists current allergen labelling as unverified).
