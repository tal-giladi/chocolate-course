## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Maltitol content of a 54% no-sugar-added bar (~40%, "rest is mostly maltitol"): inferred from the cocoa %; Callebaut does not publish it.
- "A child weighs less, so the same bar affects them more": general reasoning from per-kg limits (EFSA erythritol, GRN 400 allulose); no maltitol per-kg study opened.
- Maltitol/erythritol do not caramelise or brown like sugar (07.1 says so too): general chemistry, no source opened. 13.2 only says substitutes "don't caramelise like it" and that allulose browns fast (Xia).
- Allulose in tempered chocolate: no manufacturer data found; lesson says so.
- Home spice-grinder milling of sweeteners: general practice.
- Date-ball and sauce shelf life (fridge, 1 week; sauce 3 days): conservative judgement from KSU aw bands; not measured.
- Raw-cocoa "don't serve to pregnant women, babies, elderly, immunocompromised without telling them": general food-safety practice; not from the NCA article.
- Freeze-dried raspberry ~30 g sugar/100 g: our calculation from fresh raspberry (USDA via Wikipedia).
- Nut-bar roasting 160 °C 10-14 min: between 02.3 (160-170 °C, 8-10 min) and OSU (150 °C, 20-30 min).
- Eating window 2-4 weeks for nut bars: judgement from Callebaut's bloom note.
- Hebrew wording of the EU polyol warning: our translation; official Hebrew not verified (marked in 13.5).
