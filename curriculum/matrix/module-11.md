## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Cell 2-5 mm wider than the item; lid 3-5 mm above the tallest item; shake test procedure (45° tilt + 3 light shakes).
- "Many bags sold as צלופן are plastic film (usually OPP)": inference from product naming; Wikipedia only says the name is genericised. The lesson says so.
- Israeli rules for food contact materials: not checked; the lesson says buy from baking/food packaging shops and look for "מתאים למזון" or the glass-and-fork symbol.
- 25 °C threshold for adding an ice pack: course rule of thumb (between 18-20 °C recommended and 33.8 °C form V melting); the lesson says so.
- Trunk temperature: not measured in the cited study; the lesson says so.
- Bonbon cost breakdown (7 g chocolate + 5 g filling ≈ 0.90 ₪), sprinkles 1 ₪, paper cups/tissue 0.50 ₪, ribbon 0.50 ₪, divided box 6 ₪ (9) and 10 ₪ (16): estimates, labelled as such in the worked example.
- Box weights (5: 50-110 g, 9: 90-130 g, 16: 160-240 g): computed from course item weights (08.2, 05.1, 06.2, 04.4).
- Sprinkles choking hazard under age 4: general child-safety practice (same as 08.2).
- Gel-pack contact makes a cold spot and condensation; "keep below about 25 °C, not cold": reasoning from Callebaut condensation guidance.
