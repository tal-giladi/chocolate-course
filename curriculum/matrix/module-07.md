## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Salted caramel recipe quantities (100 g sugar, 30 g water, 30 g glucose, 100 g cream, 25 g butter, 1.5-2 g salt) and the two-stage temperatures: 175-180 °C before cream (within the ATK/Pastry Arts ranges) and 104-106 °C final for a soft piping caramel; 118-120 °C for a chewy bar caramel. General confectionery practice; no opened source gives these exact finals.
- Water activity of a soft 105 °C caramel filling: no published measurement found; the lesson says so explicitly and gives no number. Shelf life "up to about two weeks, cool, closed box" for caramel bonbons is conservative judgement aligned with 05.2.
- Cream warmed to 60-70 °C: practice; ATK/GBC only say "warm".
- Homemade praliné 60:40 (100 g nuts, 67 g sugar) modelled on Valrhona 60% hazelnut; caramel 170-175 °C; grinding times 8-12 min in a food processor; "praline bonbons ~3-4 weeks at 14-18 °C" (judgement; Valrhona's 12 months is factory-sealed).
- "About two thirds of a hazelnut is fat": derived from Valrhona 60% hazelnut praliné = 38% fat.
- Home gianduja 80 g praline 60% + 60 g dark 55% ≈ 34% nuts / 42% sugar / 24% cocoa: my arithmetic, compared to Valrhona 34/34/28.
- Pectin: why acid goes in last (pre-gelling in the pot) is my explanation of the Perfect Purée order; "106 °C is clearly above 60% solids" is reasoning (boiling-point elevation), no Brix-temperature table cited; fruit purée "85-90% water, aw near 1" marked "ידע כללי" in the table.
- Ganache aw "about 0.85-0.9" in 07.3 refers to module 6 (06.4 / m06 water-activity diagram); jelly "about 0.6-0.75" from Fontana's 0.50-0.75.
- Citric acid as a substitute for tartaric at the same weight: not sourced (forum suggests different strengths); lesson offers either at 1.5 g.
- Vegan marzipan without egg white: the lesson says no professional numeric recipe was found. Aquafaba nougat: pointed to module 12, no numbers.
- Peanuts as kitniyot for some on Pesach: stated as "אצל חלק מהנוהגים", ask the rabbi; link to 10.4 as plain text.
- Bar assembly (base 40 g chocolate, nougat 1 cm, caramel 118-120 °C, cut 2.5x7 cm, enrobe): own design from the sourced components.
