## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Why touch-dry chocolate bends (part of the cocoa butter crystallised, part still liquid): explanation built from module 3 crystal forms; not stated in an opened source.
- Room-temperature effect on the window (18-22 °C comfortable, 27-28 °C barely sets, 17 °C very short): practice, consistent with 04.3; no opened source gives numbers.
- Scraper angle ~30° for cigarettes; start 3-4 cm from the edge (Torres says 1½ in): angle from forum snippets in search results, not opened.
- Microwave pulse of 5 s at 50% for the peeler block: Williams Sonoma gives 50% but no time.
- Parchment cone folding steps, 25-30 cm square cut diagonally, 1 mm opening, hold tip 1 cm above paper: standard pastry practice; ICE/Saveur pages not accessible.
- Cone thickens because a small mass cools and keeps crystallising; hair dryer 2-3 s: reasoning + practice.
- Lettering joined at one point to lift as one piece; piping on cocoa powder (adapted from Callebaut's powder-coated flakes).
- Circumference = diameter × 3.14 + 2 cm overlap; height + 2-2.5 cm (Food Heaven says ~1 in).
- Collar thickness 1.5-2 mm and ~100 g for a 16 cm cake: estimate.
- Leaving acetate on the collar in the fridge until serving protects it from condensation: reasoning from the Callebaut sugar-bloom page, common pastry practice.
- Composition tips (one focal point, groups of 3 or 5), inserting pieces 1 cm into cream, glue with ganache: general decorating practice.
- Hot knife warmed in tap water, not on a flame: general safety advice.
