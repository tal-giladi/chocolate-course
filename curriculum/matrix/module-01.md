## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Typical composition numbers in `assets/m01-chocolate-types.svg` (dark 70: 38/32/29/1; milk 35; white 28; compound 12% cocoa, 33% fat, 53% sugar) are illustrative, built from the EU minimums and LibreTexts ranges; the lesson says so.
- White couverture "usually 26-34%": from product rows in `references/kashrut-guide.md` (26%, 28%, 34%), not from a manufacturer statement.
- Dark 70% ≈ 30% sugar, 55% ≈ 45%: arithmetic assumption that the rest is mostly sugar (small lecithin/vanilla).
- Compound melt target 40-45 °C, max 50 °C: from LibreTexts (coat ~40 °C, ≥50 °C harmful); "package instructions win" added.
- Glass holds heat longer than plastic; wooden spoons absorb water and odours: general kitchen practice.
- 100 ml water = 100 g at room temperature: physics, rounded.
- Tasting at 18-22 °C, mild → strong order, not when very hungry: professional practice; marked in 01.5 as not from a measured source.
- Compound melts "at or above mouth temperature" and feels waxy: inferred from LibreTexts melting range (35-37 °C) plus general descriptions; LibreTexts itself does not describe taste.
- Starter kit 300-600 ₪: course plan estimate (plan §2), not a priced basket.
- מד"א 101 and epinephrine use: general Israeli emergency knowledge; FDA says evaluate need for epinephrine and seek care.
- Caffeine in high-% dark for young children: general knowledge, phrased only as "small piece".
- "חלב ישראל" definition, קטניות/לציטין: taken from `references/kashrut-guide.md` (already verified there), not re-sourced.
