## Statements without a direct source (judgement or general practice)

- Shell thickness "about 1-2 mm": general practice; no source states a number.
- Home tweaks: hair-dryer 5-10 s on the mould; home fridge 5 min after the shell and 10-15 min after capping (derived from Cacao Barry 5 min / CakeFlix ≤15 min).
- Praline filling ratio 60 g praline : 25 g chocolate (~2.5:1): derived from Callebaut's ruby recipe (~2.6:1 nut paste to chocolate), not a published home recipe.
- Simple ganache 60 g dark : 50 g cream: a common soft ratio for piping; module 6 teaches ratios. Shelf life "up to about two weeks at 14-18 °C" for this ganache without glucose/butter: conservative judgement (CakeFlix gives 3-4 weeks for her improved recipes).
- Praline bonbons "several weeks": reasoning from near-zero water; no trial cited.
- DIY colour: "a pinch, ~0.5-1 g per 20 g cocoa butter": no manufacturer dosing found; lesson says start small and add.
- Guichon (28 °C) vs RCI (32-33 °C) for coloured cocoa butter: presented as two methods; the reconciliation (pre-tempered butter warmed below form V melting point vs full temper) is my reasoning.
- "Don't heat the mould after colouring": reasoning from Guichon's heat-gun warning and form V melting point.
- Israel and E171: no official MoH statement found (search 2026-11-10); the permitted-additives list was not checked.
- Spoilage signs (bubbling, sour/alcoholic smell, new leaking) as a discard rule: general food-safety practice.
